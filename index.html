I am Kavyaa Somasundaram, a final-year PhD candidate in Human–Robot Interaction and Applied Mathematics at Örebro University, Sweden. My work explores how humans make mistakes, slips, and lapses when remotely operating robots in complex settings, and how robots can intelligently step in to prevent failures without taking control away from the human.

My research focuses on human error mitigation in teleoperation, particularly in underwater robot navigation and inspection tasks. I develop frameworks for intelligent disobedience and goal-aware error reasoning, where a robot can detect when an operator’s actions diverge from the task goals, communicate why that might be risky, and offer or execute safer alternatives. This involves integrating video-based analysis, error taxonomies, mixed-method user studies, and multimodal large language models.

Alongside my PhD, I have also worked on low-rank tensor and matrix methods for image reconstruction and motion capture data completion, and I am a co-creator of Healthish, a tool aimed at making healthcare recruitment more human-centric and efficient by combining structured and voice-based screening to build a rich picture of candidates before interviews.

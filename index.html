Hello there! I am Kavyaa Somasundaram, a researcher shaped by curiosity and a fascination with "why humans act the way they do". I'm a final-year PhD candidate at Örebro University, Sweden, in collaboration with SAAB, working at the intersection of human behaviour, AI and robotics.

My work spans HRI/HAI, user research, human factors, behaviour modelling, multimodal reasoning and computer vision. I’m also interested in research related to child–AI interaction, AI-supported mental / digital wellbeing, and healthcare applications of AI.

At the heart of all of this is, I love building and designing systems that centers around people - their quirks, intentions and imperfections to support them in a meaningful way.

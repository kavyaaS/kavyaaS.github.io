Mixed-methods Research · Game-based Evaluation

Human Errors in Teleoperation: Game Study with Intervention Design

👥 40 participants 🧪 Wizard-of-Oz + A/B 🎮 Unreal Engine 5 📄 ACM THRI papers

Research question

How do users respond to autonomous interventions during teleoperation, and how do feedback, control, and intervention style affect performance, trust, workload, agency, and acceptance?

Methods

Wizard-of-Oz study, A/B testing, behavioural metrics, questionnaires, post-task semi-structured interviews, and task-based evaluation metrics, hypothesis testing, equivalence testing, descriptive and inferential stats.

My role

End-to-end research cycle: Designed two user studies and RQ's, built the UE5 simulation, created task scenarios, conducted pilot studies, recruited and conducted the participant study, analysed mixed-methods data, and translated findings into design recommendations.

Outputs / impact

Two ACM THRI paper's, thesis contribution, study protocol, demo, and design recommendations for feedback timing, explanation style, user control, and intervention type and acceptance respective to error types.

Key takeaway: Autonomous support (ID) can help users recover from errors without detoriating their performance. The type of intervention depends on the cognitive characteristics of the human error type (No one size fits all!), but acceptance depends strongly on timing, explanation, and perceived agency. Different types of trust dimensions contribute to different categories of human erros.

Conversational UX · Voice AI · Usability Testing

Conversational UX Research for Sara Voice AI

👥 20+ interviews 🧪 10+ usability tests 🎙 Voice AI 🛠 Figma + Miro + ElevenLabs + Video surveys

Research question

How can a voice-based AI assistant support healthcare recruitment while maintaining clarity, comfort, trust, and usefulness for both candidates and recruiters?

Methods

Stakeholder interviews, workflow mapping, concept testing, conversational UX design, Figma prototyping, and usability testing.

My role

Led user research, product discovery and AI development, mapped healthcare recruitment workflows, designed conversational flows, created prototype concepts, and led usability testing.

Outputs / impact

Workflow maps, Figma prototypes for website and app, conversational scripts, ElevenLabs voice agent prototyping, voice interaction flows, usability findings, and product direction toward a scalable SaaS workflow.

Key takeaway: The value of voice AI was not just automation, but reducing coordination burden while preserving candidate comfort and recruiter confidence. Adaptable voice agents are key to integration.

Online Study · AI Evaluation · Human Benchmark

Online Evaluation of Goal-Aware AI Framework Accuracy and Explanations

👥 90 participants 🌐 Online experiment 📊 Human-AI comparison 🤖 Explanation evaluation

Research question

Can a goal-aware AI system detect human error situations and generate explanations that align with human reasoning and decision making about task goals, context, and error type?

Methods

Online study, human benchmark comparison, Qualtrics, Prolific, Sunnet survey, inter-rater coding, confusion matrices, AI output evaluation, and LLM-as-judge as a secondary check.

My role

Designed the evaluation workflow, Developed the GAER AI model, structured prompts, participant-facing materials, coding protocol, and analysis pipeline.

Outputs / impact

Evaluation framework, human-AI comparison, explanation analysis, online study materials, and manuscript under review.

Key takeaway: The AI model came very close to the human decision making benchmark. Error detection needs goal context and explanation. Slip error type was a special case where both participants and AI model struggled to make accurate decisions.

Field UX · Visualization · Operator Support

Field UX and Visualization Support for Underwater Search

🌊 Field research 🗂 Data planning 💬 Stakeholder workshops 📡 Sonar + camera 🖥 Interface concept

Research question

How can sonar and camera information be presented to support underwater robot operators during low-visibility search tasks?

Methods

Field observation, contextual inquiry, data collection planning, stakeholder feedback sessions, knowledge-transfer workshops, co-design discussions, and interface concept development.

My role

Conducted field research and observations, helped plan data collection scenarios, worked with rescue-diving stakeholders to identify relevant data needs, supported knowledge-transfer sessions around sonar interpretation, and translated these insights into operator-centred output and interface recommendations.

Outputs / impact

Data collection strategy, stakeholder-informed research insights, reconstructed operator-support concepts, visualization recommendations for sonar-camera outputs, and design directions for more usable AI-assisted decision support in low-visibility underwater search.

Key takeaway: In noisy and low-visibility environments, useful AI support depends not only on detection accuracy, but on how outputs are interpreted, communicated, and integrated into operator workflows.
